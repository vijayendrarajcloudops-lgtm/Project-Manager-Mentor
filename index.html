💡 A nuance worth knowing: certs land differently at Ford vs. at Tier 1 tech companies
Your existing AIPMM CPM is ISO-accredited, which formal HR promotion committees at large enterprise and manufacturing companies — exactly Ford's profile — recognise and weight explicitly. That makes it a genuinely strong lever to name directly in an internal promotion conversation. Tech-native companies (Google, Flipkart, Razorpay and similar) tend to weight shipped portfolio and outcomes far more than additional generic PM certifications. Practical implication: lean on your CPM for the internal Ford case; lean on technical GCP/Databricks certs plus your outcome stories for external Tier 1 moves.
